CalSnap - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 18, 2026

Introduction

CalSnap ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the CalSnap mobile application.

Information We Collect

Camera and Photos: CalSnap uses your device camera to scan images of food for nutritional analysis. Photos are processed temporarily for the sole purpose of identifying food items and calculating nutritional data. We do not store, upload, or share your photos with any third party.

Health and Nutrition Data: The app tracks nutritional information such as calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat based on your meal scans. This data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any external server.

Purchase History: If you subscribe to CalSnap Premium, your purchase is processed through Apple's App Store. We use RevenueCat to verify your subscription status. We do not have access to your payment details.

How We Use Your Information

All data collected by CalSnap is used solely to provide the core functionality of the app:

  • Scanning food and calculating nutritional information
  • Tracking your daily macro and calorie goals
  • Verifying your subscription status

Data Storage

All nutritional data and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate external servers that store your personal data. If you delete the app, all locally stored data will be permanently removed.

Third-Party Services

CalSnap uses the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat: For subscription management and purchase verification.
  • Apple App Store: For processing in-app purchases.

These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties for advertising, marketing, or any other purpose.

Children's Privacy

CalSnap is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your information at all times. You can delete all data by simply uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
